Who we are

One family of believers,
across every denomination.

We are an interdenominational fellowship with a simple conviction: that lives are transformed through Jesus Christ, and that people should be loved, discipled and equipped to fulfil godly purposes — spiritually, mentally and financially.

Our vision The four pillars Our work Leadership

Our mission

  1. To win souls for Christ — get souls saved.
  2. To build a loving fellowship where every person is valued.
  3. To disciple believers spiritually, mentally and financially.
  4. To empower young people through mentorship and education.
  5. To raise productive, Kingdom-minded leaders.
  6. To support churches through media and technology.

Our vision

Four pillars. One direction.

To raise a generation characterised by purity, empowered by God’s power, established in Kingdom-minded prosperity, and committed to continuous progress.

01

Purity

Raising people of God in purity.

Holiness, integrity and self-control — a life shaped by God’s character, including what is done when no one is watching.

1 Peter 1:15–16
02

Power

Conscious of God’s power within.

Empowered by the Holy Spirit to serve, witness and influence responsibly in the spiritual, political, economic and leadership spheres.

Acts 1:8
03

Prosperity

Kingdom-minded and financially empowered.

Not display, but stewardship — work, wisdom, generosity and the capacity to support the Kingdom and carry others.

Deuteronomy 8:18
04

Progress

Always advancing, never stagnant.

Growth in Christ, in knowledge, in skill and in service — moving forward with the Gospel into every sphere.

2 Peter 3:18

What we do

There is a place for everyone.

Evangelism & outreach

Schools, villages, markets and communities. Souls saved, then discipled — never merely counted.

Student mentorship

Mentoring secondary-school students, supporting WAEC and JAMB preparation, and standing with them through admission.

Business & financial training

Practical training that helps members create value, earn responsibly and support themselves and others.

Leadership development

Training people to become confident, articulate and capable in every room they enter.

Prayer & the Holy Spirit

Prayer as practice, not theory — seeking the empowerment of the Spirit for life and witness.

Church media & technology

Helping churches across Nigeria tell the Gospel story with excellence, clarity and reach.
